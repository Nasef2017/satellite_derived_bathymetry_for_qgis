## 📖 1. Abbreviations & Terminology

*   **SDB (Satellite-Derived Bathymetry):** The science of calculating water depth using the color (spectral reflectance) of the seabed captured by satellite sensors.
*   **OSW (Optically Shallow Water):** The coastal zone where sunlight successfully hits the sea bottom and bounces back to the satellite. Bathymetry is **only** valid here.
*   **ROI (Region of Interest):** A custom polygon you provide to tell the algorithm: *"Sample the pixels inside this shape to calibrate your thresholds."*
*   **NIR (Near-Infrared):** A band of light that water absorbs heavily. It's used to detect the edge of water, land, and deep ocean.
*   **NDWI (Normalized Difference Water Index):** A mathematical formula used to easily distinguish water from dry land.
*   **Residuals (Prediction Error):** The exact difference (in meters) between what the ML model guessed and the true depth measured by the ICESat-2 laser.
*   **MSI (Morphological Stability Index):** A normalized index indicating how physically stable or volatile the seabed is over multiple years.

### 📁 Phase_01_Preprocessing/
Contains the results of water masking, sunglint correction, and optically shallow water extraction.

> 💡 **Best Practice & Workflow Recommendation (Optimization of Water Masking & OSW Filters)**:
> Satellite imagery varies widely in water clarity, coastal turbidity, bottom reflectance (sand vs. seagrass vs. reefs), and sun-glint. **Users are strongly advised to run Module 01 (Preprocessing) independently first** to test and compare the available Water Masking and OSW methods on their specific scene before launching the full end-to-end MasterFlow:
> 
> 1. **Testing Water Masking Methods**:
>    * **`Otsu (Automatic NDWI)`**: Fast, optimal for sharp, clear contrast between land and water.
>    * **`Smart Hybrid (Dynamic Auto)`**: Best for complex coastlines, wet intertidal sands, and tidal flats.
>    * **`3 Indices Equation (NDWI, MNDWI, NWI)`**: Multi-spectral synthesis ideal for difficult coastal edges and inland lagoons.
>    * **`Manual NDWI Threshold`**: Precise threshold control when automated methods over- or under-segment.
> 
> 2. **Testing OSW (Optically Shallow Water) Filtering Methods**:
>    * **`0. Automated Knee-Point Extinction [Recommended]`**: Automatic physics-based cutoff detecting the true optical extinction point on NIR absorption.
>    * **`1. Turbidity-Invariant Log-Ratio Extinction`**: Recommended when dealing with river plumes, estuarine sediment, or high suspended particulate matter.
>    * **`2. Multi-Otsu / GMM Spectral Clustering`**: Recommended for complex reef lagoons and heterogeneous benthic environments.
>    * **`3. Automatic (NIR Percentile Fallback)`**: Allows manual tuning of the deep-water percentile (e.g., 5% to 15%).
>    * **`4. Manual Polygon ROI / 5. Custom OSW Polygon`**: Gives full manual control by defining deep-water samples or exact shallow vector boundaries.
> 
> 👉 **Visual Quality Check in QGIS**: Load `01_Land_Water_Mask.tif` and `06_Final_OSW_Mask.tif` on top of your satellite image in QGIS to verify that shallow coastal areas are fully preserved while deep ocean waters are completely masked out.

*   **`01_Land_Water_Mask.tif`** (Binary Raster: 0/1):
    *   **What it is:** The boundaries of water. `1` = Water, `0` = Land/Clouds.
    *   **Why it matters:** Acts as a cookie-cutter to ensure processing is only done on water, saving time and memory.
*   **`02_Sunglint_Corrected.tif`** (Float32 Raster):
    *   **What it is:** Original satellite image but wave-induced sun reflections have been mathematically subtracted.
    *   **Why it matters:** Removes surface glare to reveal the true seabed color underneath.
*   **`03_Feature_Stack.tif`** (Float32 Raster):
    *   **What it is:** The ML dataset containing mathematically calculated Log-Ratios and Indices (e.g., NDWI, Blue/Green, Green/NIR).
    *   **Why it matters:** Linearizes light decay in water. The ML model learns depth exclusively from this file.
*   **`04_DeepWater_Threshold_Stats.csv`** (Spreadsheet):
    *   **What it is:** The mathematical limits showing the exact Mean, Standard Deviation, Cutoff value, and pixel counts computed by the active OSW method (`0. Automated Knee-Point Extinction`, `1. Turbidity-Invariant Log-Ratio Extinction`, or `2. Multi-Otsu / GMM Spectral Clustering`).
    *   **Why it matters:** Documents the exact physical and statistical thresholds used to isolate deep ocean waters dynamically.
*   **`05_ShallowWater_Pixel_Mask.tif`** (Binary Raster: 0/1):
    *   **What it is:** The raw filter result of pixels that passed the Deep Water thresholding (`1`=Shallow, `0`=Deep).
    *   **Why it matters:** Shows the raw statistical cutoffs before any spatial smoothing is applied.
*   **`06_Final_OSW_Mask.tif`** (Binary Raster: 0/1):
    *   **What it is:** The clean footprint of the Optically Shallow Water (OSW) mask.
    *   **Why it matters:** Polished using automated Knee-point inflection detection, connected-component topological cleaning, and internal gap filling to represent the true bathymetric prediction zone.
*   **`07_OSW_Boundary_Polygon.gpkg`** (Vector Polygon):
    *   **What it is:** The vector boundary outlining the clean OSW mask.
    *   **Why it matters:** Automatically clips the final ML depth maps in Phase 03 and Phase 04 so they don't predict fake depths in the deep ocean.
    *   **Coordinate System:** Automatically inherits and embeds the Spatial Reference System (SRS/CRS) of the source satellite image to ensure seamless alignment and prevent GDAL cutline warnings during subsequent clipping phases.

### 🌊 📁 Coastal Dynamics Analysis (Standalone Module 06)
Contains the results of the volume calculations and shoreline migration tracking.
*   **`Temporal_Analytics_Report.html`** (Interactive Report):
    *   **What it is:** A comprehensive HTML report detailing volumetric changes, dredging volumes, target sub-region ROI metrics, uncertainty mode settings, and morphological stability with a color-coded spreadsheet.
    *   **Quality Control (QC) Value:** The Target ROI metrics within this report allow users to validate the model's real-world accuracy by confirming that calculated erosion/accretion quantities align with expected physical changes (e.g., known dredging volumes).
*   **`Quantile_Uncertainty_[Year].tif`** (Float32 Raster - generated when Quantile Regression mode is active):
    *   **What it is:** Spatially-adaptive uncertainty raster map ($\sigma$) estimated using Quantile Regression ($q_{\text{lower}}$ and $q_{\text{upper}}$) fitted on ground-truth ICESat-2 data.
*   **`Full_Process_Log.txt`** (Text File):
    *   **What it is:** Real-time log file recording all execution details, warnings, and processing steps throughout the Temporal Intelligence pipeline run.
*   **`Volumetric_Erosion_Accretion_[First]_[Last].tif`** (Float32 Raster):
    *   **What it is:** Measures the Magnitude and Direction of physical sand volume change (in cubic meters).
    *   **Methodology:** Uses a robust **Linear Regression (Time-Series Trend)** across all provided years. This mathematically filters out deep-water random noise and calculates the true rate of sand movement, identifying dredging (Erosion) and shoaling (Accretion). 
    *   **Mathematical Equation (Long-term Trend):**
        *   $Z_{diff} = Z_{arr} - \bar{Z}$
        *   $T_{diff} = T - \bar{T}$
        *   $\text{Slope (m/yr)} = \frac{\sum (T_{diff} \times Z_{diff})}{\sum (T_{diff}^2)}$
        *   $\Delta Z = \text{Slope} \times (\text{Year}_{last} - \text{Year}_{first})$
    *   **Auto-Detection:** Features **Auto-Detection** for Depth (positive) vs Elevation (negative) inputs via median analysis `np.nanmean(z1[valid]) > 0`, ensuring flawless sand tracking regardless of data format.
*   **`Morphological_Stability_Index_[First]_[Last].tif`** (Float32 Raster):
    *   **What it is:** Measures the Volatility and Consistency of the seabed, regardless of whether it is eroding or accreting.
    *   **Methodology:** A normalized index from 0 to 1 (0 = Highly Unstable, 1 = Highly Stable).
    *   **Mathematical Equation:**
        *   $MSI = 1.0 - \frac{\sigma_{true}}{|\mu| + 0.5}$
        *   Where $\sigma_{true} = \sqrt{\max(0, \sigma_{obs}^2 - \bar{\sigma}_u^2)}$ ($\bar{\sigma}_u$ is the mean uncertainty of the inputs).
*   **`StatCD_Uncertainty_Thresholding`** (Applied during Volume Calculation):
    *   The algorithm enforces a **Statistical Level of Detection (StatCD)** before calculating volumes. Any pixel where the magnitude of change $|\Delta Z|$ is less than the combined uncertainty $U_{comb}$ is forced to 0 (No Change).
    *   **Mathematical Equations (By Mode):**
        *   **Classical Mode (Z-score 1.96 $\sigma$):** $U_{comb} = 1.96 \times \sqrt{\sigma_1^2 + \sigma_2^2}$
        *   **MAD Mode (Median Absolute Deviation):** $U_{comb} = 1.4826 \times \text{MAD}$
        *   **Quantile Mode:** $U_{comb} = \frac{q_{upper} - q_{lower}}{1.349}$

## 🏆 4. Customizable Auto-ML Benchmark Scoring & Model Selection

Bathymetrix-AI features a streamlined, multi-criteria Auto-ML ranking and selection engine with **3 compact, intuitive UI controls**:

### 🎯 1. Model Selection Strategies (`SCORE_SELECTION_STRATEGY`)
* **Winner Stability (Monte Carlo Sensitivity Analysis) [Default]:**
  * Performs $N$ Monte Carlo simulation rounds with varying equation weights sampled within variation bounds. The model that wins the highest percentage of rounds (`Stability %` and `Wins`) is selected as the overall champion.
* **Highest SDB Composite Score (0–100):**
  * Selects the model with the highest single composite score calculated using the active normalized weights.
* **Highest $R^2$ Accuracy:** Selects the model with the highest coefficient of determination ($R^2$).
* **Lowest RMSE (Root Mean Squared Error):** Selects the model with the minimum vertical metric error ($\text{m}$).
* **Lowest wMAPE (%):** Selects the model with the lowest weighted mean absolute percentage error.
* **Lowest |Bias| (Zero-Mean Residual Offset):** Selects the model closest to zero systematic vertical offset ($\text{m}$).
* **Lowest MAE (Mean Absolute Error):** Selects the model with the minimum mean absolute error ($\text{m}$).

### ⚖️ 2. Checkbox Metric Selection with Auto-Balancing (`SCORE_METRICS`)
Users can select which evaluation metrics participate in the score calculation using a multi-select checkbox list:
* ☑️ **$R^2$ Accuracy (Correlation & Explained Variance)** `[Default: Checked]`
* ☑️ **RMSE (Root Mean Squared Error)** `[Default: Checked]`
* ☑️ **wMAPE (Weighted Mean Absolute Percentage Error)** `[Default: Checked]`
* ☑️ **|Bias| (Zero-Mean Residual Offset)** `[Default: Checked]`
* ⬜ **MAE (Mean Absolute Error)** `[Default: Unchecked]`

> [!TIP]
> **Automatic Weight Balancing:** When metrics are checked or unchecked, the engine **automatically distributes and normalizes the weights proportionally** to sum exactly to 100% (1.0). Unchecked metrics are strictly excluded with weight `0.0`.

### 🎛️ 3. Optional Custom Score Matrix & Settings (`SCORE_CUSTOM_CONFIG`)
An optional parameter field allowing custom weight assignments and Monte Carlo sensitivity tuning without needing to edit complex templates:
* **Default Value:** `""` (Empty — automatically balanced via `SCORE_METRICS` checkboxes).
* **How to Customize:**
  * **Custom Weight Overrides:** Specify only the metrics you want to assign explicit weights to, e.g. `"R2: 50, MAE: 50"` or `"MAE: 40, RMSE: 30, R2: 30"`.
  * **Auto-Normalization:** You don't need to manually calculate percentages to reach 100 — the engine automatically scales active weights to sum to 1.0 (100%).
  * **Simulation Tuning:** Modify rounds and variation tolerance directly (e.g. `"Rounds: 50, Variation: +/-20%"`).
  * **Seamless Checkbox Integration:** When selecting `MAE` from `SCORE_METRICS`, the system immediately includes MAE in the auto-balancing equation proportionally without requiring manual string edits.
